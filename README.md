## 👩‍💻 About Me

> *"Turning raw data into meaningful insights — one model at a time."*

I build **production-grade, AI-powered full-stack web applications** with a focus on **social impact** — healthcare, food redistribution, and local business empowerment across India. My work combines clean ML pipelines with real deployment: FastAPI backends, Next.js frontends, and OpenRouter-powered AI layers shipped to Vercel and Render.

- 🎓 **B.Tech CSE (Data Science)** — RTMNU, Nagpur · 4th Year
- 🤖 Specialising in **Full-Stack AI · ML Pipelines · NLP · Multilingual Systems**
- 🌍 Building for **social impact** — healthcare, food systems, local commerce
- 🗣️ Multilingual by design — **English · Hindi · Marathi** in every applicable product
- 🎯 Goal: **AI Engineer / Data Scientist at a product-driven company**

**Open To:** AI Engineering Roles · Data Science Internships · Full-Time Opportunities · Research & Open Source
